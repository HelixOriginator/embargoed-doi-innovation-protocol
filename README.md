# Embargoed DOI Innovation Protocol (`embargoed-doi-innovation-protocol`)

An open architectural specification for a **Dark / Embargoed DOI Registry** designed to provide cryptographic priority-locking and NDA-bound adjudication for unaffiliated and grassroots inventors.

---

## 📌 Executive Overview

Grassroots innovators and unaffiliated researchers face a critical barrier in the traditional intellectual property (IP) ecosystem:
1. **High Cost of Patent Protection:** Early-stage patent drafting, legal advisory, and filing fees often cost $\approx$ ₹1,00,000+ ($1,500–$3,000 USD), pricing out independent creators.
2. **The Prior Art Paradox:** Publishing research openly to prove priority destroys patent eligibility under the strict "First-to-File" rules of major patent offices.

The **Embargoed DOI Innovation Protocol** resolves this paradox by creating a zero-knowledge priority timestamp using cryptographic payload hashing (SHA-256) linked to a dark/embargoed Digital Object Identifier (DOI). This establishes immutable, legally defensible prior art while preserving absolute confidentiality until patent application or public release.
