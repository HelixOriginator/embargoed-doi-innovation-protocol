Helix Originator Specification

Dark / Embargoed DOI Registry

A Cryptographic Priority-Locking Framework for Unaffiliated & Grassroots Innovators

🔒 Sealed Priority

Uses SHA-256 cryptographic hashing to lock invention timestamps without public metadata exposure.

🛡️ Legal Safety

Preserves strict "First-to-File" patent novelty by preventing premature public prior-art disclosure.

🌐 Grassroots Funding

Connects independent researchers directly to NDA-bound adjudication panels and patent-filing grants.

Architecture & Workflow

1
Cryptographic Intake: Technical payloads are hashed locally. The immutable fingerprint (SHA-256) is committed to the registry database.
2
Embargoed DOI Issuance: A dark DOI is allocated. Metadata remains completely sealed while establishing legal time-priority.
3
Adjudication & IP Grant: Anonymized, NDA-bound reviewers evaluate merit to provide filing micro-grants prior to national patent lodge.
